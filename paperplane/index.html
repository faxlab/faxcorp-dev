My Paper Plane Is Better

Throw a paper plane through a city. Your flight becomes a track. Everyone races your line.

Steam · November 2026 · free demo at Next Fest, October 19-26

Wishlist on Steam Privacy policy

How it works

  • Your flight is the track. Drop into the city, pick a plane and throw. Every flight you record becomes a track with a name, a ghost and a leaderboard, published the moment you land. Add hoops mid-flight to shape the challenge.
  • Race everyone else's flights. Pick any track, start where its author started and chase the top ten. Their ghosts fly beside you, so there is always someone to beat and never anyone to wait for.
  • Real paper, real physics. Lift, drag, trim, and a wing that folds when you pull too hard. Every number is a paper decision, not a stat sheet.

Modes

  • Free Fly - no timer, no hoops. Throw, and see where the city takes you.
  • Race - hoops in order, best time wins.
  • Glide - one throw, how far and how long.
  • Balloons - pop them against the clock.

Two-minute flights, built for a controller, tuned to run smooth on Steam Deck.
