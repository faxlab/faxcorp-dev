How it works

  • Fly free - drop into the map, find your spot, pick a launch speed, throw.
  • Every throw is a track - your flight path and the hoops you place along it are published automatically for everyone.
  • Race the world - pick any track, count down, chase the top-10 ghosts, take the record.
