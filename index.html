About

FAXCORP is the studio of Kajus Janulevičius, a 3D generalist and real-time graphics developer based in Kaunas, Lithuania. Working in 3D and game development since 2012, publishing Unreal Engine environments and tools since 2018, shipping games on Steam since 2019.

Everything here is made by one person, with the studio's own tools. Since September 2026 the studio operates as MB „Faxcorp“, a Lithuanian small partnership. Company details are below.
